I study how genetic variation shapes cardiometabolic traits, particularly blood lipids, through GWAS, rare-variant analyses, and Bayesian methods for assessing replication. I contribute to [TOPMed](https://topmed.nhlbi.nih.gov) and the [Global Lipids Genetics Consortium](https://www.lipidgenetics.org).
